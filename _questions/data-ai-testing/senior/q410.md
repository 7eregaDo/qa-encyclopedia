## The Intent
The interviewer is checking whether you understand that recommendation quality is multi-dimensional and that the standard ML accuracy metrics tell only part of the story. At senior level, they want to see that you can design evaluation that reflects the full commercial and ethical requirements of the system, not just its technical performance.

## The STAR Script
**Situation:** My team operated a content recommendation engine on a media platform. The system had been evaluated exclusively on click-through rate and had achieved a 12% CTR — above the industry benchmark. But the editorial team had raised two concerns: the recommendations were showing users only content they had previously engaged with (filter bubble behaviour), and content from newer creators was never being recommended despite high editorial quality scores. I was asked to redesign the evaluation framework.

**Task:** I needed to design an evaluation strategy that measured recommendation quality across all commercially and editorially relevant dimensions, not just click-through rate.

**Action:** I defined a five-dimension evaluation framework and operationalised each with specific metrics. Accuracy: I retained CTR as a production metric but added precision@k on a held-out user-item interaction test set (did the recommended items appear in the user's future interactions?). Coverage: I measured catalogue coverage — the percentage of items in the catalogue that appeared in at least one recommendation over a 7-day period. The existing system had 23% coverage — 77% of the catalogue was never recommended. I set a minimum coverage threshold of 60%. Diversity: I measured intra-list diversity (ILD) — the average pairwise content distance between items in a recommendation list, using content embedding similarity. Low ILD indicated a filter bubble. I set a minimum ILD threshold calibrated against editorial judgement of "varied" recommendation lists. Novelty: I measured the average popularity rank of recommended items — highly popular items have low novelty. I wanted the system to balance popular items with lower-popularity discoveries, and I set a novelty target of at least 30% of recommendations being outside the top 10% most popular items. Fairness (item producer equity): I measured the Gini coefficient of recommendation exposure across creator accounts — a Gini of 0 means equal exposure, 1 means all exposure to one creator. The existing system had a Gini of 0.71 — highly concentrated on established creators. I set a target of below 0.5 and introduced a minimum exposure floor for creators above an editorial quality threshold.

**Result:** The existing system failed four of five dimensions. The catalogue coverage and novelty shortfalls were addressed through changes to the retrieval stage — introducing candidate diversity sampling. The fairness issue required a more significant intervention: a creator exposure reranking layer. After six weeks of retuning, the system met all five dimension thresholds — and CTR remained within 1.5 percentage points of the original, demonstrating that quality breadth and accuracy were not in fundamental conflict.

## Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Catalogue coverage** | The proportion of all available items that appear in at least one recommendation over a defined time period — low coverage indicates the system ignores most of the catalogue |
| **Intra-list diversity (ILD)** | A metric measuring the average dissimilarity between items within a single recommendation list — low ILD indicates a filter bubble |
| **Novelty** | A recommendation quality dimension measuring how often the system introduces users to items outside their existing consumption patterns or the most popular items |
| **Filter bubble** | A recommendation failure mode where users are only shown content similar to what they have previously consumed, limiting discovery and reinforcing existing patterns |
| **Gini coefficient** | A measure of inequality in a distribution — used here to measure how unevenly recommendation exposure is distributed across item producers |
| **Precision@k** | A retrieval metric measuring the proportion of recommended items in the top k that are relevant — based on held-out future interactions |
| **Creator exposure floor** | A minimum guaranteed recommendation exposure for creators meeting a quality threshold — a fairness mechanism preventing total marginalisation of new or minority producers |
